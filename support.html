I paid and Pro didn't unlock

Open Ballast, go to the paywall, and tap Restore purchase. That re-checks your Apple Account and is the fix in almost every case, including after reinstalling or moving to a new phone, as long as you use the same Apple Account you bought with.

If it still doesn't unlock, email me. Include the receipt Apple sent you and I'll sort it out. You will not be asked to pay twice.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by me: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Ballast. Cancelling there stops the renewal, and Pro stays on until the period you already paid for runs out.

Lifetime is a single payment. There is nothing to cancel, and nothing renews.

What happens to my shield if Pro ends?

Locks release cleanly and app shields come off. The adult-website filter is free, so it stays under your own control. Nothing that needs a subscription is left stuck on your phone.

How do I turn Shield off?

With no commitment lock, turn it off in the Shield tab. With a lock, you wait out the cool-off you chose, or enter the code your trusted person holds. If they can't be reached, a 24-hour wait always works. A finished wait gives you 15 minutes to turn things off; after that the lock is back, so a wait can't be saved up for later. Ballast has no server and can't reset a code for you, and it doesn't need to: nobody is ever trapped.

A phone's owner can also undo this in Settings by removing Ballast's Screen Time permission. The Shield screen will say the shield is down, without judgement. What filters can and can't do.

The filter blocked a normal site, or missed one

This is Apple's filter, and Apple doesn't document exactly what counts as adult, so no list is complete. If it matters, tell me what happened and I'll tell you what I can do. Be aware that turning the filter on also disables Safari private browsing, by Apple's design.

How do I back up or move to a new phone?

In the app, make a backup file and save it through the share sheet somewhere you control, such as Files or your own cloud storage. Restore from that file on the new phone. Both are free. There's no Ballast account and Ballast keeps no copy, so keep that file somewhere safe, and remember it holds your private entries. Pro comes back through Restore purchase. Shield settings belong to the device and aren't in the backup, so set them up again on the new phone.

How do I get my data out?

In the app: Settings → Export my data. That produces a JSON file of your start date and settings, urges and slips, check-ins, reasons and habits, and you choose where it goes. Nothing is uploaded. Urge plans, course answers, steps, weekly reviews and the picture aren't in it; a backup is the full copy.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything Ballast stores lives on your phone, so that removes it from the phone; there is no copy on a server, because there is no server. Any backup files you saved are yours to delete, and an iCloud or computer backup of the phone made earlier can still hold Ballast's data until you delete that backup. For the purchase record Apple and RevenueCat hold, email me and I'll request its deletion on your behalf.

Does Ballast work without a connection?

Yes. Everything except buying or restoring Pro works with no signal at all, including the whole urge flow.

When to look for more help

Ballast is a self-tracking and educational tool, not treatment. None of these is a diagnosis, but any one is a fair reason to book a conversation: you've tried to cut back several times and it keeps coming back; it's eating into sleep, work or time with people; it has become the main way you deal with stress or low mood; or you feel low or anxious most days.

  • A therapist. Look for someone who works with CBT or ACT and is comfortable talking about sex.
  • Your GP or family doctor. A good first stop if you're unsure. They can check what else might be going on and refer you on.
  • SMART Recovery. Free peer meetings, online and in person, built on practical CBT-style tools.
  • SAA. A twelve-step fellowship with free meetings in many countries and online.

If things feel very dark: if you are having thoughts of harming yourself, contact your local emergency services or a crisis line now. In the US, call or text 988. Elsewhere, findahelpline.com lists free lines by country.

Ballast is an on-device iPhone app (iOS 26 or later) for adults who want to quit porn and compulsive masturbation (PMO). It offers a one-tap iOS adult-website filter, if-then urge plans and progress that a slip cannot erase. The full urge flow, slip support and the filter are free. There is no account and no server. Personal entries stay on device; Apple and RevenueCat process purchases. It uses skills from CBT and ACT. It is not treatment.
