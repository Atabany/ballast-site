How to write one that holds up

  • Name the moment you can actually notice. "When I'm in bed and reach for my phone" beats "when it gets hard".
  • Make the "then" physical. A place, an object, a first step. "Phone on the kitchen charger" works at 1am. "Be stronger" doesn't.
  • Keep it small enough to happen. If it takes planning to carry out, it's too big.
  • Write it on a calm day, and change it whenever you learn something. A plan that didn't hold has told you something useful.
  • Start with one or two. Your most common situation matters most.

Does this actually help?

If-then plans are also called implementation intentions. A 2006 meta-analysis by Gollwitzer and Sheeran found a medium-to-large effect (d≈0.65) on reaching goals of many kinds. They haven't been tested on pornography specifically, so this is a reasonable bet, not an established treatment. The full evidence summary covers what's been tested directly.

When to look for more help

Ballast is a self-tracking and educational tool, not treatment. None of these is a diagnosis, but any one is a fair reason to book a conversation: you've tried to cut back several times and it keeps coming back; it's eating into sleep, work or time with people; it has become the main way you deal with stress or low mood; or you feel low or anxious most days.

  • A therapist. Look for someone who works with CBT or ACT and is comfortable talking about sex.
  • Your GP or family doctor. A good first stop if you're unsure. They can check what else might be going on and refer you on.
  • SMART Recovery. Free peer meetings, online and in person, built on practical CBT-style tools.
  • SAA. A twelve-step fellowship with free meetings in many countries and online.

If things feel very dark: if you are having thoughts of harming yourself, contact your local emergency services or a crisis line now. In the US, call or text 988. Elsewhere, findahelpline.com lists free lines by country.

Questions

Is this tool private?

Yes. It runs entirely in your browser. There is no account or analytics, and the page's security policy blocks network requests. Plans are only kept if you tick the box, and then only in this browser.

What is an if-then plan?

A decision made in advance, in the form "if this situation happens, then I will do this specific thing". Psychologists call it an implementation intention. It means you don't have to decide in the moment.

How many plans should I write?

Start with one or two for your most common situations. A short list you remember does more than a long list you don't.
